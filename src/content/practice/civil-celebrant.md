Couples in Hong Kong can marry outside a Marriage Registry, with a civil celebrant officiating at the venue and time they choose. Mr. Tsang is a Civil Celebrant of Marriages.

We handle the legal formalities — giving notice of intended marriage, conducting the ceremony, and registering the marriage afterwards — so that you can concentrate on the day itself.
