faqs:
  - q: What happens if someone dies without a will?
    a: The estate is distributed to relatives in a fixed order set by Hong Kong's intestacy rules, and the family must apply for letters of administration before the assets can be dealt with. A will lets you decide who inherits and who is in charge.
  - q: How long does probate take?
    a: A straightforward application is often completed within a few months of the papers being lodged. It takes longer where the Probate Registry raises queries or the estate includes assets outside Hong Kong.

A properly drafted will spares your family uncertainty at a difficult time. We take the time to understand your circumstances and prepare a will that reflects what you actually want.

When someone has died, we guide executors and family members through the application for a grant and the administration of the estate. Where an estate is disputed, our litigation team can act.
