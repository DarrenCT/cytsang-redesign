Insolvency moves quickly, and the first decisions often matter most. We advise creditors on the most effective way to recover what they are owed, and debtors on how to respond when demands and petitions arrive.

The firm handles bankruptcy and winding-up matters as part of its litigation practice, and gives directors and individuals a realistic view of their position and their options.
