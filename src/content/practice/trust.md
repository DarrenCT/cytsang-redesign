faqs:
  - q: Do I need a trust, or is a will enough?
    a: It depends on what you want to achieve. A will takes effect on death; a trust can hold and manage assets during your lifetime and afterwards. We explain the differences in the context of your own family and assets.
  - q: What does a trustee have to do?
    a: A trustee must manage the trust assets in the interests of the beneficiaries and in line with the trust deed. The duties are serious, and it is worth taking advice before accepting the role.

A trust can provide for family members, protect assets for the next generation, and give certainty about how wealth is managed. It needs to be set up carefully to do what is intended.

We advise those creating trusts, as well as trustees and beneficiaries who need to understand their position under an existing arrangement.
