A well-prepared application is the surest way to avoid delay. We identify the right category for your circumstances, assemble the supporting evidence, and deal with the Immigration Department on your behalf.

We assist individuals and families relocating to Hong Kong, as well as employers sponsoring staff from overseas and the Mainland.
