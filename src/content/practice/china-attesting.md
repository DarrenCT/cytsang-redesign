Mainland authorities, courts, and counterparties generally require Hong Kong documents to be attested by a China-Appointed Attesting Officer — a Hong Kong solicitor appointed by the Ministry of Justice of the People's Republic of China — before they will be accepted. Mr. Tsang holds that appointment.

After attestation, documents are sent to China Legal Service (H.K.) Ltd. for sealing and transfer. We handle that step for you and tell you at the outset how long the whole process is likely to take.
