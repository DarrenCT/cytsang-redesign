Documents signed or issued in Hong Kong often need to be notarised before an overseas authority, bank, or court will rely on them. A Notary Public verifies identity, witnesses signatures, and certifies documents so they can be recognised abroad.

Mr. Tsang is a Notary Public. We confirm the receiving country's requirements before your appointment, so the document is accepted the first time.
