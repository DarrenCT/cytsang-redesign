Good commercial documents prevent disputes before they start. We draft and negotiate agreements with an eye on how they will hold up if they are ever tested — a perspective that comes from a practice with litigation at its core.

We act for PRC state-owned enterprises, multinational corporations, and leading corporations on non-contentious matters, giving direct, commercially minded advice rather than a list of legal caveats.
