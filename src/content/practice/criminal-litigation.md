Being investigated or charged is one of the most stressful experiences a person can face. We explain plainly what is happening, what the prosecution must prove, and what your options are, and then prepare your case thoroughly.

Criminal litigation is a core part of the firm's practice. We advise from the moment an investigation begins and represent defendants through trial, sentencing, and appeal.
