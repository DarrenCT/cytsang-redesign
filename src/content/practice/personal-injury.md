An injury can affect your health, your income, and your family all at once. We act for accident victims to recover the compensation the law provides — for pain and suffering, lost earnings, medical expenses, and future needs.

Where an injury happens at work, there are usually two routes to compensation: the no-fault employees' compensation scheme and a common law claim for negligence. We advise on both and pursue them together where appropriate.
