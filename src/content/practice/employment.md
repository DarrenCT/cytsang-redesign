Most employment problems are easier to resolve early. We help clients understand their rights and obligations under the contract and the Employment Ordinance, and find a practical way forward before positions harden.

Where a dispute cannot be avoided, we advise on claims arising from dismissal, unpaid entitlements, restrictive covenants, discrimination, and the handling of personal data.
