    a: A divorce in Hong Kong is granted by the Family Court, but where both spouses agree, much of the process is dealt with on paper. Arrangements for children and finances can often be settled by agreement or mediation rather than at a contested hearing.

Family disputes are personal before they are legal. We aim to resolve them with as little conflict as the circumstances allow — through negotiation and mediation where possible, and firm representation in court where it is needed.

We advise on the full course of matrimonial proceedings, from the decision to separate through to final orders on children and finances, and we keep you informed of likely cost at each step.
