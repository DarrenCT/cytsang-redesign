faqs:
  - q: My trade mark is registered elsewhere. Is it protected in Hong Kong?
    a: Not automatically. Trade mark rights are territorial — registration in Mainland China or another country does not by itself protect a mark in Hong Kong, which maintains its own register.
  - q: Someone is copying our product. What should we do first?
    a: Gather evidence of the copying and of your own rights, and take advice before contacting the other side. The right first step — a letter, a negotiated undertaking, or court proceedings — depends on what you own and what you want to achieve.

Intellectual property is often a business's most valuable asset and its least protected. We help clients secure their rights properly and act quickly when those rights are infringed.

Our intellectual property work covers both sides of the practice: advisory matters such as registration, licensing, and assignment, and contentious matters involving copyright, designs, patents, and trade marks.
