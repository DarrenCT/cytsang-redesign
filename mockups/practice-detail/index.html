Practice Area

Civil Litigation & Dispute Resolution

Pragmatic representation in Hong Kong's courts and tribunals — from complex commercial disputes to construction, arbitration, and contentious probate.

Overview

Disputes are costly, distracting, and rarely straightforward. Our litigators focus on the outcome that matters to you — whether that is a negotiated settlement, a favourable judgment, or a swift resolution that lets you move on.

We act for PRC state-owned enterprises, multinational corporations, and individuals, bringing the same care to a high-value commercial claim as to a personal matter. At every stage we give you a clear, honest view of where you stand and what each option is likely to cost.

Common questions

How long does a civil claim take in Hong Kong?

It depends on complexity and whether the matter settles. Many disputes resolve before trial; we give you a realistic timeline at the outset and update it as the case develops.

Will my dispute go to trial?

Most do not. We actively explore mediation and settlement, which are often faster and less costly, and reserve trial for when it is the right path.

Can you act in both English and Chinese proceedings?

Yes. We advise and appear in matters conducted in English, Cantonese, and Mandarin, and prepare documents in all three.
