About the firm

曾重瑜律師事務所於二〇〇七年在香港成立，客戶包括國有企業、跨國公司及個人。我們致力以最具成本效益、切實可行的方案，為客戶迅速解決問題。

Founded in Hong Kong in 2007, C.Y. Tsang & Co. is a litigation-led practice committed to clear, cost-effective advice — from complex commercial disputes to personal injury and criminal matters.
