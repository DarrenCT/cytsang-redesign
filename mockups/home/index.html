The Firm

A litigation practice built on clear, cost-effective advice.

Founded in Hong Kong in 2007, C.Y. Tsang & Co. acts for PRC state-owned enterprises, multinational corporations, and individuals alike — from complex commercial disputes to personal injury and criminal matters. Our commitment is simple: solve the problem, and solve it well.

Practice Areas

Where we act for our clients.

Our lawyers handle both contentious and non-contentious matters across a broad range of disciplines.

Civil Litigation & Dispute Resolution

Complex commercial disputes, construction, arbitration, and contentious probate.

Corporate & Commercial

Joint ventures, restructuring, commercial agreements, loan and IP documentation.

Family & Matrimonial

Matrimonial proceedings, children matters, and related financial disputes.

Intellectual Property

Copyright, design, patent, and trademark disputes — contentious and advisory.

Personal Injury & Compensation

Accident claims and employees' compensation, acting for victims and the legally aided.

Notarial & Attesting Services

China-Appointed Attesting Officer and Notary Public services for cross-border needs.

Also advising on conveyancing & property, employment, immigration, insolvency & bankruptcy, trust, wills & probate, and criminal litigation.
